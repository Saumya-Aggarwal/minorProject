# Research 27 Sep 2026: meta-whatsapp-onboarding

> Raw findings from the 27 Sep research run (read-only agents). The fact-check pass did not run (session limit); four key claims were checked by hand afterwards: GPT-6 Luna price and function-calling rule (its release date is 18 May 2026, not 22 Sep), Kimi line-up and retirements, Meta charging service messages from 1 Oct 2026, Shopify's `shopifyapp` package. Everything else: re-check against the linked source before relying on it. Decisions drawn from this are in /plan.md.


- [high] (1. Phone number eligibility) The number must be owned by the business, have a country and area code (short codes are not allowed) and be able to receive an SMS or a voice call. A number that is active on WhatsApp Messenger or the WhatsApp Business app has to have that WhatsApp account deleted before it can be registered. A banned number must win an appeal first.
  Registration asks for a code sent by SMS or voice call (request_code with code_method SMS or VOICE). A number can be added from App Dashboard > WhatsApp > API Setup, from Meta Business Suite or from WhatsApp Manager, and after that you must call the register endpoint. For a pilot the practical choice is a new prepaid SIM used only for the brand, so the friend's personal chats are not lost.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers
- [high] (1. Landline / toll-free) Landline and toll-free numbers can be registered. If the number sits behind an IVR, the IVR must accept calls from international numbers and pass the verification call to a person who can read out the code.
  For a landline, choose VOICE as the verification method.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- [high] (1. Numbers per unverified portfolio) A new or unverified business portfolio can register up to 2 business phone numbers. The cap rises to 20 automatically after business verification, or once the portfolio reaches the 2,000 messaging limit.
  When the cap rises, Meta sends a Business Suite notification and a webhook carrying max_phone_numbers_per_business.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers
- [high] (1. Two-step verification PIN / register) Registration is POST /{phone-number-id}/register with {messaging_product:'whatsapp', pin:'<6 digits>'}. If the number has no two-step PIN yet, this call creates one; if it has one, you must send the current PIN. The endpoint allows 10 requests per number in any 72 hours, and going over returns error 133016 and blocks registration for 72 hours.
  Two-step verification cannot be turned off through the API. A forgotten PIN is changed in WhatsApp Manager > phone number > Two-step verification > Change PIN. The optional data_localization_region parameter (for example 'IN') keeps data stored locally.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/registration
- [medium] (1. Display name) The display name is checked against Meta's display-name guidelines, and the name_status field reports APPROVED, AVAILABLE_WITHOUT_REVIEW, PENDING_REVIEW, DECLINED, EXPIRED or NONE. The docs say messages can be sent whatever the approval status is, and that the name is verified when the number reaches a higher messaging limit. It can be changed 10 times in 30 days, and after a change is approved the number must be re-registered within 14 days.
  The guidelines require the name to be clearly related to the business and its branding, for example matching the Shopify store name and domain. The Meta Help Center page with the full guidelines (facebook.com/business/help/757569725593362) did not render for me, so the exact formatting rules were not checked.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/display-names
- [high] (2. Messaging limits (unverified)) A new or unverified portfolio can deliver to 250 unique WhatsApp users outside a customer service window in any rolling 24 hours. The limit belongs to the portfolio and all its numbers share it. Messages sent inside a customer service window do not count.
  Only template sends count against this limit, such as abandoned-cart reminders and order updates sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message. Bot replies to customers who wrote first do not count.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits
- [high] (2. How limits increase) There are three ways to reach 2,000: (a) verify the business, (b) have a partner verify it, or (c) deliver 2,000 messages outside service windows to unique users within 30 days using high-quality templates. After that, the limit moves up one tier (10K, 100K, then unlimited) within 6 hours if quality is high and the portfolio used at least half of its current limit in the last 7 days.
  A webhook is sent when the limit rises. The old 1K tier no longer exists; the ladder is 250, 2,000, 10K, 100K, unlimited.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits
- [medium] (2. Business verification documents (India)) For India, Meta's per-country list accepts a GST registration certificate, Udyog Aadhaar/Udyam, a Shop & Establishment certificate, a PAN card of the business, a utility bill, a business bank statement, a business licence or a certificate of incorporation. The legal name on the document must exactly match the name in the business portfolio. Rejections often happen because that legal name does not appear on the business website.
  The official Meta Help Center pages would not render (only the Hindi title came back), so this list comes from BSP documentation that reproduces Meta's list (360dialog, Wati). Documents must be official, current, uncropped and in colour. For an unregistered sole seller, personal documents such as a personal PAN or personal bank statement generally do not prove a business name. The simplest route is a free Udyam registration, then uploading the Udyam certificate. Decisions are usually reported within about 48 hours but can take longer.
  src: https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/resources/meta-business-verification
- [high] (2. Udyam registration) Udyam (MSME) registration is free, online and paperless, and works on self-declaration. An Aadhaar number is enough and no documents are uploaded. PAN and GST details are pulled automatically from government databases.
  Only the official portal may be used; paid look-alike sites are scams.
  src: https://udyamregistration.gov.in/
- [high] (3. Template categories) A utility template must be non-promotional AND either specific to or requested by the user (their order, account or transaction) or essential to them. Order confirmation, shipping updates, backorder or exchange notices and payment reminders are utility. Abandoned-cart reminders ('You left {{items}} in your cart... Checkout now') are explicitly marketing, and so is any mixed utility-plus-promotion message.
  Meta checks the declared category and now moves templates to MARKETING automatically (allow_category_change is the default behaviour). Approved utility templates are re-checked regularly; normally 1 day's notice is given before a switch, but none is given if the business has been warned for category misuse.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/updates-to-pricing/new-template-guidelines
- [medium] (3. Template review and limits) Template review can take up to 24 hours, and only APPROVED templates can be sent. An unverified portfolio can have 250 templates per WABA; a verified portfolio with an approved display name can have up to 6,000. Templates are rated for quality and can be paced, paused or disabled on poor feedback, and templates unused for 12 months are archived.
  
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview
- [high] (3. Media headers and buttons) The header can be TEXT (up to 60 characters, 1 variable), IMAGE, VIDEO, DOCUMENT or LOCATION. The body can be up to 1,024 characters and the footer up to 60. A template can have up to 10 buttons in total: at most 2 URL buttons (each allows one dynamic variable appended to the URL), at most 1 phone button, up to 10 quick replies and at most 1 copy-code button. Catalog MPM/SPM buttons are also available.
  For abandoned carts, use a marketing template with an image header and a URL button whose dynamic suffix carries the Shopify checkout path. The sample for a media header is uploaded with the Resumable Upload API when the template is created.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/components
- [high] (3. Per-user marketing limits (India)) WhatsApp may cap how many marketing templates one user receives from all businesses combined, based on that user's engagement. The cap applies in India: only the EEA, UK, Japan and South Korea are excluded, and US numbers receive no marketing templates at all. A blocked send comes back in the webhook as error 131049; Meta advises waiting at least 24 hours before retrying.
  There is no fixed per-user number; the cap changes dynamically. Budget for some abandoned-cart reminders never arriving.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/marketing-templates/per-user-limits/
- [medium] (4. India rates (Meta rate card)) Meta charges for each delivered message, not per conversation, since 1 July 2025. India INR rates (rate card effective 1 July 2026, same on the 1 October 2026 card): marketing Rs 0.8631, utility Rs 0.1150, authentication Rs 0.1150, authentication-international Rs 2.4971, service from 1 Oct 2026 Rs 0.1150. 18% GST is added on top.
  The official INR CSV is linked from Meta's pricing page, but I could not open it directly; the numbers come from two independent BSP copies of Meta's card (chatmaxima, whautomate), which agree. Utility and authentication get volume discounts only at 25M+ messages a month. The authentication-international rate applies only to senders based outside India with 750K+ such sends. India billing localisation started 1 Jan 2026; eligible WABAs must be migrated to INR by 31 Dec 2026.
  src: https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/
- [high] (4. VERIFIED: service/utility inside the 24h window stop being free on 1 Oct 2026) The claim is VERIFIED. Meta's own page says that from 1 October 2026 service (non-template) messages and utility templates sent inside the customer service window move from free to charged. Both are billed per message at the market's utility/authentication rate, with no volume tiers.
  Service messages had been free since 1 Nov 2024, and utility templates inside the window since 1 Jul 2025. Messages in the 72-hour free entry-point window (opened by Click-to-WhatsApp ads or a Facebook Page CTA) stay free to deliver. The Meta Business Agent (Meta's own AI) is a separate product billed by tokens from 1 Aug 2026 and is not what we use.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages
- [high] (4. Free tier and payment-method deadline) From 1 Oct 2026 each business phone number gets a free tier of 1,000 delivered service messages per month; one-to-one and group sends share it, and unused messages do not roll over. Any directly integrated business without a payment method on file by 30 Sep 2026 has service-message delivery stopped from 1 Oct 2026.
  These sentences appear in Meta's pricing docs according to the site-restricted search results, but the page as I fetched it did not show them. 360dialog, respond.io and others report the same terms. Cost implication for Munim: each photo, intro and footer is a separate delivered message, and the bot currently sends about 4 to 6 per answer. For example, 40 chats a day at 6 messages each is about 7,200 a month, of which about 6,200 would be billable at Rs 0.115 plus GST, roughly Rs 840 a month billed to the brand's card.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing
- [high] (5. Opt-in) Meta's opt-in requirements: (1) clearly state that the person is opting in to receive communication from the business; (2) clearly state the business's name; (3) comply with applicable law. The opt-in may be general rather than WhatsApp-specific. Listed channels are SMS, website, phone IVR, and in person or on paper. Separate opt-ins per category (for example order updates versus offers) are recommended, and opt-out requests must be honoured on or off WhatsApp.
  The Business Messaging Policy only allows contacting people who (a) gave you their number and (b) gave opt-in permission for later messages. Automated chats must also offer a prompt, clear, direct way to reach a human (hand-off in chat, phone, email, web form or store). The current opt-in doc does not list 'inside a WhatsApp thread' as a channel, so use a website/checkout consent line that names the brand and log it with a timestamp.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in
- [high] (6. System User permanent token) Steps: Business Settings > System users > Add (choose Admin or Employee role) > Assign assets: the app (Manage app) and the WABA (full control) > Generate token, pick the app, choose expiration 'Never' and select business_management, whatsapp_business_management and whatsapp_business_messaging. An admin system user can reach every WABA owned by or shared with the portfolio; an employee system user needs each WABA granted.
  Meta's docs call these tokens 'long-lived'. The 'Never' expiry choice is described by several BSP guides but not stated outright on the Meta page I read.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/access-tokens/
- [high] (6. Webhooks and subscribed_apps) Set the callback URL and verify token in the App Dashboard and subscribe to the 'messages' field. Each WABA must also be subscribed to the app with POST /{WABA_ID}/subscribed_apps using the bearer token, and GET on the same edge confirms it. A per-WABA callback can be set by adding override_callback_uri and verify_token to that POST, and a per-number one through the phone number's webhook_configuration. Meta checks the number override first, then the WABA override, then the app default.
  Payloads can be up to 3 MB, and Meta retries a non-200 response for up to 7 days. The app must be in Live mode, because some webhooks are not sent in Dev mode, and Live mode needs a privacy policy URL. Each app has its own app secret, so X-Hub-Signature-256 must be checked with that tenant's secret.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/override
- [high] (7. Can an app in OUR portfolio serve HIS WABA?) No, not without Tech Provider status. Meta states that without Advanced Access to whatsapp_business_management, 'API calls that use this permission on WABAs not owned by your business return error code 200'. Advanced Access requires business verification of OUR business plus App Review, which is the Tech Provider route.
  The workable manual setup is to create the Meta app INSIDE the friend's business portfolio. The developer, added as an admin, creates the app with that portfolio selected, so the app, the WABA and the system user all belong to one business and Standard Access is enough, with no App Review. Sharing his WABA with our portfolio as a 'partner' does not get around the error 200 restriction. Munim's backend has to store app_id, app_secret, token, WABA id and phone_number_id per tenant.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/get-started-for-tech-providers
- [high] (7. What Tech Provider / Embedded Signup adds later) Becoming a Tech Provider requires verifying our own business, App Review for Advanced Access to whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management, and building Embedded Signup; access verification is no longer required. Embedded Signup lets a client create and share their WABA and number with our one app in a pop-up, and we then use Business Integration System User tokens. Until we are verified and App Reviewed, onboarding is capped at 10 new business customers per rolling 7 days; after that it is 200 per week. Tech Provider clients attach their own payment method.
  
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/overview/
- [high] (7. Embedded Signup deprecation) Embedded Signup v2 and v3 will be deprecated on 15 October 2026 and integrations must move to v4. Most are converted automatically, but ones using only_waba_sharing, marketing_messages_lite or coex must be upgraded by hand before that date.
  This does not affect the manual setup; any future Embedded Signup work should start on v4.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2026/05/14/embedded-signup-v4/
- [high] (7. Coexistence) Coexistence keeps the same number working in the WhatsApp Business app and on the Cloud API at once. It is only available through Embedded Signup by a Solution Partner or Tech Provider. It needs WhatsApp Business app 2.24.17 or later, runs at a fixed 20 messages per second, disables disappearing messages, view-once, live location and broadcast lists, has no group support, and can sync the last 6 months of history. Messages sent from the app stay free; messages sent through the API are billed.
  This matters if the friend's store number is already on the WhatsApp Business app. Without Tech Provider status, that account has to be deleted from the app, or a new number used.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users
- [high] (8. Downloading customer images and voice notes) The webhook's image.id or audio.id goes to GET /{media-id}?phone_number_id=..., which returns url, mime_type, sha256 and file_size. The URL expires after 5 minutes. The file is then downloaded with GET <url> and the header 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'; the request fails without the token. Size limits: images JPEG/PNG 5 MB; audio AAC/AMR/MP3/M4A/OGG (Opus) 16 MB; video 3GPP/MP4 16 MB; documents 100 MB; stickers WebP 100 to 500 KB. Media IDs that arrive in webhooks stay valid for 7 days.
  Since 12 Nov 2025 Meta has been gradually adding image.url / audio.url to the webhook, a URL that can be fetched directly with the token, so the lookup call can be skipped where it is present. Check sha256 after download. Download inside the background task, not the webhook handler, so the handler still returns 200 at once.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/media
- [high] (8. Voice-note detection) An incoming voice note arrives as type 'audio' with audio.voice = true and mime_type 'audio/ogg; codecs=opus'.
  Groq, the project's current LLM provider, offers Whisper Large V3 Turbo at $0.04 per audio hour. It accepts OGG directly with a 25 MB limit on the free tier, so voice notes can be transcribed without converting the file.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/messages/audio
- [medium] (Side note: Kimi for image/voice) The Kimi K2.6 API (api.moonshot.ai/v1) is OpenAI-compatible, supports tool calling, and accepts images as base64 image_url. Its docs show no audio input or speech-to-text endpoint, so switching the LLM to Kimi does not provide voice-to-text.
  Voice notes would still need an ASR step such as Groq Whisper. For image search, a vision LLM can describe the photo, and the description then goes through the existing retrieval; image embeddings are another option. Check Moonshot's data-use terms against Meta's rule below.
  src: https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/guide/kimi-k2-6-quickstart
- [medium] (9. Platform terms: AI use and data) Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform terms (effective 23 Sep 2026) bar 'AI Providers' from the platform when AI is the primary thing offered. A business may still use an AI provider for secondary purposes such as customer support. WhatsApp data may not be used to create, train or improve any AI model, except fine-tuning exclusively for the business's own use.
  Munim's shop assistant is the allowed kind of bot. Pick LLM providers (Groq, Kimi) whose API terms say they do not train on your inputs, and keep each brand's feedback data used only for that brand.
  src: https://www.facebook.com/legal/Meta-Terms-for-WhatsApp-Business-Platform
- [high] (9. Graph API version in the code) The repo calls WhatsApp Cloud API v21.0, which Meta retires on 21 January 2027. The latest version is v26.0, released 29 Jul 2026, and v25.0 is supported until 29 Jul 2028.
  Bump the version constant before the multi-brand launch.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/
- [medium] (9. Billing for the friend's WABA) Businesses billed in India (Sold-To country India in Billing Hub) can only use INR. A payment method must be attached to the WABA in Billing Hub / WhatsApp Manager, and from 1 Oct 2026 service messages are not delivered without one. Monthly invoicing is for spend above about $10K a month, so the pilot needs a card.
  The Meta Help Center pages on adding a card did not render, so whether Indian cards need e-mandate or international-transactions enabled is unverified.
  src: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing

# RECOMMENDATION
Onboarding checklist for the friend's number (manual setup: no Tech Provider, app created inside HIS portfolio). Today is 27 Sep 2026; Meta starts charging service replies on 1 Oct 2026.

A. What the friend does (about 1.5 hours of his time, spread over 1 to 2 days):
1. Facebook login: use his real, established account with two-factor authentication on. (10 min)
2. Business portfolio: create one at business.facebook.com under the exact name that will appear on his documents and in the Shopify footer. Add a business email and the store's website. Optionally make two-factor authentication required in Security Center. (15 min)
3. Add the developer: Settings > People > invite the developer's email with Full control (admin). The developer accepts. (5 min)
4. Get a number that is not on WhatsApp: a new prepaid SIM used only for the brand is best (about 1 day for KYC activation). If he uses a number already on WhatsApp or the WhatsApp Business app, that account must be deleted first and its chats are lost; keeping both needs coexistence, which only Tech Providers can offer. A landline also works, verified by voice call.
5. Be reachable for the SMS or voice code while the developer adds the number. (5 min)
6. Add a card in INR in Billing Hub / WhatsApp Manager > Payment methods as soon as the WABA exists. Without it, service replies stop from 1 Oct 2026. (10 min)
7. Shopify changes: publish a privacy policy page, show his legal/brand name in the footer, and add a checkout or sign-up consent line such as "Get order updates and offers from <Brand> on WhatsApp", logged with a timestamp. Offer a way to reach a human (phone or email) in the bot. (30 min, with the developer)
8. Optional, needed to scale beyond the pilot: register on Udyam (free, Aadhaar OTP, about 20 min), then start Meta Business Verification in Security Center with the Udyam certificate (plus GST if he has it) and a legal name that matches exactly (about 20 min to submit; the decision often takes about 2 days and can take longer). This raises the limit from 250 to 2,000 unique users a day outside the 24h window, from 2 to 20 numbers, and from 250 to 6,000 templates. The pilot does not need it, because replies to customers who write first are not limited.

B. What the developer does (about half a day of work, plus up to 24 hours waiting for template approval):
1. Accept the invite and register as a Meta developer. Create a Business-type app with the WhatsApp use case, choosing HIS portfolio. An app in our own portfolio would get error 200 on his WABA without Advanced Access. (20 min)
2. WhatsApp > API Setup > Add phone number: set a display name that matches the brand and website, a category and a timezone, then verify by SMS or voice. (15 min)
3. Call POST /{phone_number_id}/register with {messaging_product:"whatsapp", pin:"6 digits"}. Keep the PIN in a password manager; only 10 register calls are allowed per 72 hours. (5 min)
4. System user: Business Settings > System users > Add (Admin) > assign the app (Manage) and the WABA (full control) > Generate token with expiry Never and the scopes business_management, whatsapp_business_management and whatsapp_business_messaging. Store it per tenant, together with the app secret, WABA id and phone_number_id. (15 min)
5. Webhooks: in App > WhatsApp > Configuration set an HTTPS callback (for example /webhook/{tenant}) and a verify token, and subscribe to 'messages'. Then call POST /{WABA_ID}/subscribed_apps and confirm with GET. Check X-Hub-Signature-256 with this app's secret. (30 min)
6. App Settings > Basic: add the privacy policy URL, icon and category, then switch the app to Live, since some webhooks are not sent in Dev mode. (10 min)
7. Raise the code's Graph API version from v21.0 (retired 21 Jan 2027) to v25 or v26, then retest sending and webhooks. (1 hour)
8. Templates: order_confirmed, shipped and exchange_update as UTILITY with no promotional wording. abandoned_checkout as MARKETING, with an image header and a URL button whose dynamic suffix is the Shopify checkoutUrl path. Approval takes up to 24 hours. Handle error 131049 (per-user marketing cap in India) by not retrying for 24 hours. (1 hour + wait)
9. Media: for images and voice notes, GET the media id to get a URL valid for 5 minutes, then GET that URL with the bearer token, or use the webhook's url field where it is present. Limits are 5 MB for images and 16 MB for audio. Treat audio.voice=true (ogg/opus) as a voice note and send it to Whisper, for example on Groq; Kimi has no speech-to-text endpoint. Fetch media within 7 days. (half a day to a day of coding)
10. Keep costs down: from 1 Oct, each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, then Rs 0.115 plus 18% GST each. Marketing costs Rs 0.8631 plus GST (about Rs 1.02) and utility Rs 0.115 plus GST. Cut the messages per bot turn (currently intro, then 3 to 4 photos, then footer). If the customer wrote within the last 24 hours, send the abandoned-cart reminder as a normal chat message instead of a marketing template.
11. End-to-end test from a real phone: an inbound text, image and voice note, a template sent outside the 24h window, and hand-off to a human.

C. Later, at about 3 or more brands: become a Tech Provider. That means verifying our own business, App Review for Advanced Access with screen-recorded demos, and building Embedded Signup v4 (v2 and v3 stop on 15 Oct 2026). Brands would then onboard themselves in a pop-up onto our one app, coexistence becomes possible, and each brand pays Meta directly. Until we are verified and reviewed, onboarding is capped at 10 new brands per 7 days.

# OPEN QUESTIONS
- A WABA created after 30 Sep 2026 without a payment method: does it get the 1,000 free service messages, or is service delivery blocked until a card is added? Meta's wording only covers accounts that lack a payment method on the deadline. Safe course: add the card before the first live message.
- Do utility templates sent inside the 24h window come out of the 1,000-message free tier, or are they billed from the first one? The sources only describe the free tier for service messages.
- Does every photo message count as a separate billable service message? Per-delivered-message billing implies yes, which would make Munim's current turn (intro, then 3 to 4 photos, then footer) cost about 5 to 6 messages. Are typing indicators and read receipts free?
- Do Indian debit or credit cards work in Meta Billing Hub for WhatsApp without special e-mandate or international settings? The Meta Help Center pages did not render.
- Meta's official India document list for business verification, and whether a Udyam certificate on its own (with a matching website) is enough for a home-based sole seller. The list used here comes from BSP copies of Meta's list.
- The exact INR figures should be checked against the official INR CSV linked from Meta's pricing page; I could not open the CSV directly.
- Is in-chat opt-in ('reply YES for order updates') acceptable? The current Meta opt-in doc lists SMS, website, IVR and paper, but not a WhatsApp thread.
- Does the developer test number count toward the 2-number cap of an unverified portfolio?
- Until the display name is approved, do customers see the brand name or only the number? The docs say sending works regardless of name status.
